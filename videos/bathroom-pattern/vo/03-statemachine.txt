A sub-five-millisecond state machine runs directly on chip silicon. When the door opens from outside with the light off, the light turns on instantly before you step inside, starting a sixty-second entry timer. When you pull the door shut, occupancy latches: lights and exhaust fan stay locked on continuously, even if you are standing completely still in the shower. When you finish and open the door, a sixty-second exit timer begins, turning everything off once vacant.
