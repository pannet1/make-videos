# Auction Explainer Video Prompt

Create a clear, engaging YouTube explainer video that explains how auctions work to buyers. The video should be informative, easy to understand, and visually appealing. Use the actual EcomSense.in UI (online at ecomsense.in/auctions) as the visual reference — the real web page, colors, and branding — and use actual images from the current open auction as visual support throughout the video to make it more authentic and relatable.

## Branding & UI Reference (from the online EcomSense.in site)

- **Brand:** EcomSense.in — dark theme throughout. Wordmark rendered as `EcomSense` in white bold (font-black) with `.in` in blue-500 accent (`#3b82f6`).
- **Background:** near-black navy `bg-slate-950` (`#020617`).
- **Navigation:** black `bg-black/90` sticky bar with a bottom border `border-slate-800`, brand on the left, uppercase micro-text links (e.g. LOGIN, 10px bold, tracked-out, slate-300 on hover white).
- **Currency:** Indian Rupees, displayed as `Rs. 21,000` with thousands separators. Voiceover should say "rupees".
- **Page header:** "Live Auctions" in large bold (`text-3xl font-black`), white.
- **Section headers:** "Current Auction" / "Past Auctions" in white `text-xl font-bold`.
- **Auction cards:** glassy dark cards — `bg-slate-900/50` with `backdrop-blur-xl`, rounded corners (`rounded-2xl`), border `border-slate-800/50`.
- **Stat boxes:** three-column grid of `bg-slate-800 rounded-lg` tiles with an uppercase micro-label (slate-400) and a bold value:
  - Base Price → green-400 (`#4ade80`)
  - Highest Bid / Final Price → yellow-400 (`#facc15`)
  - Total Bids → blue-400 (`#60a5fa`)
- **Primary button:** blue `bg-blue-600 hover:bg-blue-700` rounded-lg, white bold text ("Place Bid").
- **Payment/UPI buttons:** green `bg-green-600 hover:bg-green-700`.
- **Modals:** `bg-slate-900` panels with `border-slate-700`, `rounded-2xl`, over a `bg-black/60` backdrop.
- **Warning/error text:** yellow-400 for guidance, red-400 for errors.
- **Image treatment:** product photos shown `object-contain`, `rounded-xl`, `border-slate-700`, on a `bg-slate-800/50` panel; thumbnail strip below with a blue-500 ring on the active image.

Recreate these exact colors, glass-card look, typography, and layout in the video scenes so the explainer looks like a natural extension of the real product.

## Video Structure

1. **Opening (0:00-0:16)**
   - On-screen: EcomSense.in wordmark on the `bg-slate-950` background, then the "Online Auctions" header
   - Title: "How Auctions Work: A Buyer's Guide"
   - Voiceover: "How Auctions Work: a buyer's guide. Want that laptop? Here's how to win it on EcomSense auctions. We'll cover what an auction is, how to prepare, and how to place your bid."

2. **What is an Auction? (0:16-0:34)**
   - Simple definition: A method of buying and selling goods through competitive bidding
   - On EcomSense it's a forward (ascending) auction: the highest bidder at closing wins
   - Show the current open auction card ("Dell Latitude 7420") with its product photos
   - Visual: Simple animation showing the bidding amount climbing
   - Voiceover: "What is an auction? A method of buying and selling goods through competitive bidding. On EcomSense it's a forward auction: the highest bidder at closing wins. Right now, the Dell Latitude 7420 is open for bidding."

3. **Auction Preparation (0:34-0:50)**
   - Browse the current auction and study the product photos and Base Price
   - Set your budget and maximum bid in your head
   - Make sure you have a registered account and wallet balance to cover the bid
   - Visual: Checklist animation over the stat boxes (Base Price in green)
   - Voiceover: "Prepare before you bid. Study the product photos and the base price. Set your budget and your maximum bid. Make sure you have a registered account and enough wallet balance to cover it."

4. **Your Wallet (0:50-1:09)**
   - Fund your wallet anytime through UPI — no expiry, no minimum, no lock-in
   - Withdraw your balance any time by requesting a withdrawal — funds are never trapped
   - The wallet balance is relevant only at the moment the auction is resolved
   - Everyone is encouraged to keep sufficient balance, because if the highest bidder can't cover their bid, a random participant with sufficient balance wins instead
   - Visual: Wallet card with balance, "Add Funds" (UPI) button, and "Withdraw" button
   - Voiceover: "Your wallet is your bidding fund. Top it up anytime through UPI, and withdraw your balance whenever you like — there's no lock-in. Your balance only matters when the auction is resolved. So keep enough balance — if the highest bidder can't cover their bid, a random participant with sufficient balance wins instead."

5. **During the Auction (1:09-1:34)**
   - Click the blue "Place Bid" button on the auction card
   - Your bid must be above the current highest bid AND a multiple of Rs. 100 (show the yellow-400 warning text)
   - Track the Highest Bid (yellow) and Total Bids (blue) as they update online
   - The auction ends on its expiry date — whoever holds the highest bid at close wins
   - Visual: Split screen of the card, stat boxes, and bid modal
   - Voiceover: "During the auction, click the blue Place Bid button on the auction card. Your bid must be above the current highest bid, and a multiple of one hundred rupees. Track the highest bid and the total bids online as they update in real time. The auction ends on its expiry date — whoever holds the highest bid at the close wins."

6. **After Winning an Auction (1:34-1:53)**
   - Winning bid minus wallet balance = amount due
   - Scan the UPI QR code, pay the amount due, upload your payment proof (green buttons)
   - Wait for verification; winning and payment are confirmed on the card
   - Visual: Timeline showing QR → pay → upload proof → verified
   - Voiceover: "You won! Winning bid minus your wallet balance is the amount due. Scan the UPI QR code, pay the amount due before expiry, and upload your payment proof. Can't cover it at closing? A random participant with sufficient balance wins instead."

7. **Tips for Success (1:53-2:11)**
   - Start with a smaller auction to gain experience
   - Set a max and don't get caught up in bidding wars
   - Keep enough wallet balance to cover your bid
   - Bid in clean multiples of Rs. 100 so you never get blocked
   - Visual: Quick tip icons with brief explanations
   - Voiceover: "Tips for success. Start with a smaller auction to gain experience. Set a maximum, and don't get caught up in bidding wars. Keep enough wallet balance to cover your bid, and bid in clean multiples of one hundred rupees."

8. **Closing (2:11-2:24)**
   - Recap: Browse, bid, win, pay via UPI
   - Encouragement: "The current auction is online — go check it out!"
   - Call to action: Visit ecomsense.in/auctions
   - End screen: EcomSense.in wordmark and related video suggestions
   - Voiceover: "Recap: browse, bid, win, pay via UPI. The current auction is online, so go check it out at ecomsense dot in slash auctions. Happy bidding!"

## Visual Style Recommendations
- Match the online EcomSense.in UI exactly: `bg-slate-950` backdrop, glassy `bg-slate-900/50` cards, blue-500 accent, green/yellow/blue stat values, rounded-2xl cards, font-black headings (see Branding section above)
- Clean, modern animations
- Clear, readable typography
- Simple iconography for concepts
- **Use actual images from the current open auction** (auction #5 — "Dell Latitude 7420", the only active auction at ecomsense.in/auctions; its 4 photos live at `/uploads/auction_5/1.jpeg` through `4.jpeg`). Verify via the `/api/auctions` endpoint at render time and use whichever auction has `status: "active"` and `is_expired: false`. Never mix in past/resolved auction photos.
- Real auction footage (if available) or relevant stock footage
- On-screen text to reinforce key points
- Background music: Upbeat but not distracting
- Voiceover: Clear, friendly, enthusiastic tone

## Key Points to Emphasize
- Auctions are transparent - you can see competing bids (Highest Bid + Total Bids update online)
- You control your bid, but it must be above the highest bid and a multiple of Rs. 100
- Research the current auction's photos and Base Price before bidding
- Understand all costs: winning bid minus wallet balance = amount due via UPI
- The auction is time-boxed — the highest bid at expiry wins
- All pricing shown in Indian Rupees (Rs.)

- Online shoppers curious about online laptop auctions
- Users already on ecomsense.in who haven't tried the auction yet
- Age range: 25-55
- Tone: Helpful, educational, empowering

- 2 to 2.5 minutes (8 chapters, ideal for YouTube retention)
